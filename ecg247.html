What is ECG247?

ECG247 is a medically certified wireless heart rhythm sensor used for continuous ECG monitoring over days. The advanced system works seamlessly with a dedicated app and cloud service to capture and analyze cardiac rhythm data in real-time, providing comprehensive insights into your heart health.
