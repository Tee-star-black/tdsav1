THE ESSENTIALS

What is ECG247, exactly?

ECG247 is a medically certified heart rhythm sensor used with a smartphone app. It can help record rhythm changes over several days, with results that may be shared with a healthcare professional.

Explore the official instructions ↗
01

Records beyond a single appointment

Wear the small sensor during everyday activities to capture heart rhythm information over a longer period.

02

Works with the ECG247 app

The app helps you start a test and access results. The sensor communicates with your phone during recording.

03

Supports informed follow-up

Share recordings with a doctor for assessment. Automatically flagged rhythm changes must be confirmed by a clinician.

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps. One clearer picture.

The official ECG247 app walks you through the detailed setup. Here is the process at a glance.

  1. 01

    Get the app

    Install the ECG247 app on your phone and start a test or follow your clinician's invitation.

  2. 02

    Prepare the sensor

    Follow the app to connect the sensor and apply the electrode patch to clean, dry skin.

  3. 03

    Record your rhythm

    Wear the sensor for the recommended period while keeping your phone nearby and the app running.

  4. 04

    Review the results

    Check the recorded findings and share your test with a doctor if clinical review is needed.

For precise fitting, battery and app instructions, always use the manufacturer's guide. View setup guide ↗
WHEN IT MAY HELP

Some symptoms need more than a snapshot.

A healthcare professional can advise whether longer heart rhythm monitoring makes sense for your symptoms or medical history.

Important to know

ECG247 monitors heart rhythm. It does not diagnose a heart attack or replace emergency care. Seek urgent medical help for chest pain, fainting or other severe symptoms.

Occasional palpitations

Rhythm changes that are difficult to capture during a short consultation.

Irregular or unusual pulse

More information for a clinician investigating possible rhythm disturbances.

Clinician-recommended follow-up

Extended recording when your care team needs a broader view of your rhythm.
