Before you enquire

Before you contact us

A few useful answers before your first enquiry.

How quickly will someone respond?

Send your enquiry to the team using WhatsApp or email. Response times depend on operating hours. For a non-emergency question, you can also call us.

What if I do not know which service I need?

Use the callback form and choose “Help me choose the right option.” TeleDoctorSA will route you to the most relevant starting point instead of making you guess first.

Can I ask about records or devices before I book?

Yes. Patients can enquire about ECG247 monitoring, TeleDoctorSA app access, records support, and digital follow-up before committing to a consultation.

Should I use this site for emergencies?

No. The website and callback flow are for planned support, digital care coordination, and non-emergency questions. Urgent or emergency care should use the appropriate immediate care channel.

Electronic Health Records

At our facility, we prioritize the secure digital storage of all your medical records, ensuring that they are not only protected but also easily accessible whenever you need them. This user-friendly approach allows you to effortlessly track your health progress over time, giving you a clearer view of your wellness journey.

Additionally, by digitalizing your records, any healthcare professional examining your case can quickly and effectively identify patterns or correlations from your medical history. This streamlined access to comprehensive information ultimately enhances the efficiency and accuracy of their assessments, leading to better-informed healthcare decisions and improved patient outcomes.

Legal Notice

We process personal data in accordance with the following legal and ethical principles:

1. Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA - South Africa)

All personal information is collected and processed lawfully and responsibly under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (Act No. 4 of 2013).

Our processing activities ensure that:

  • Information is collected directly from the data subject (you) unless otherwise permitted by law.
  • Data is used only for legitimate healthcare, billing, and administrative purposes.
  • Reasonable security measures are in place to protect data from loss, damage, or unauthorised access.
  • Patients may access, correct, or request deletion of their personal information at any time.

2. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Article 6(1)(b))

Where applicable, data is also processed under GDPR Article 6(1)(b), which permits processing necessary for:

  • The performance of a contract between you and MedKulula, such as subscription management or service delivery.
  • Steps taken at your request prior to entering into such a contract (for example, when you inquire or register online).

3. Healthcare Compliance

As a medical service provider, Dr Mukudu & Partners processes patient data in compliance with healthcare and ethical frameworks, including:

  • Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) guidelines on patient confidentiality and record retention.
  • National Health Act (Act No. 61 of 2003) requirements for the protection and lawful use of medical records.
  • Medical Schemes Act where information is shared with schemes for billing or benefits administration.

4. Legitimate Interests and Consent

We may process personal data under legitimate interests, such as improving our healthcare systems or responding to patient inquiries, provided this does not override your privacy rights.

Where explicit consent is required - for example, for newsletters or electronic communication - it is obtained separately and may be withdrawn at any time.

5. Cross-Border Data Protection

All personal data processed through TeleDoctorSA or MedKulula's digital systems is stored on secure servers in South Africa.

If limited cross-border transfer occurs (for example, during data backup or system support), it is conducted under conditions that ensure an equivalent level of protection as required by POPIA and GDPR.

For questions regarding data protection or to exercise your rights, please contact us through our Contact Form.
