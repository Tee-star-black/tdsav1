THE TELEDOCTORSA APP / EXPLAINED

A better way to keep up with your care.

Think of it as your personal space for the care that happens outside the consultation room. You can follow what's next, find information shared by your provider and stay connected to your healthcare team.

See the experience with us →
01PLAN & PREPARE

Know what's coming up.

Less searching for the next step. More clarity about where you need to be.

01 / APPOINTMENTS

Appointments in view

Review appointment details and, where your care provider offers it, request or manage bookings.

02 / CARE UPDATES

Relevant reminders

See appointment updates and care notifications when they are enabled for your account.

02REVIEW & UNDERSTAND

See the bigger picture.

Keep important information from your care journey easier to find.

CONNECTED HEALTH / WHERE SUPPORTED

Monitoring tools that can work with your care.

Some care pathways include readings from home health devices. Compatibility and data-sharing options depend on the devices and integrations supported by your provider.

Blood pressure

Track systolic and diastolic readings.

Blood glucose

Record or share supported glucose readings.

Oxygen saturation

Review measurements from pulse oximeters.

Temperature

Keep relevant temperature readings together.

Weight

Follow changes in weight over time.

Heart rhythm

Explore ECG monitoring when recommended.

GETTING STARTED / FOUR SIMPLE STEPS

A straightforward way to get started.

No app-store download is needed for the browser-based portal. Here is what getting connected can look like.

  1. 01

    Open the portal

    Use a phone, tablet or computer to open your patient portal and sign in.

  2. 02

    Explore your care space

    Find the appointments, records and services available to your account.

  3. 03

    Add readings when supported

    Use compatible monitoring tools as advised by your healthcare provider.

  4. 04

    Stay connected

    Follow updates and share relevant information with your care team.
