What is the TeleDoctorSA App?

The TeleDoctorSA App is your secure patient portal for connected care. Sign in to share health information with your clinical team, access your electronic health records, manage appointments, and communicate securely from your phone, tablet, or computer.

Real-Time Health Data

Send vital signs and health measurements directly to your clinicians in real-time. Sync readings from your BP monitor, glucose meter, oximeter, and more.

Appointment Booking

Schedule virtual consultations with ease. View available time slots, book appointments, and receive reminders—all within the app.

Secure Messaging

Communicate with your healthcare providers through encrypted messaging. Ask questions, share updates, and receive guidance securely.

Push Notifications

Stay informed with instant notifications for appointments, medication reminders, test results, and important health alerts.

EHR Access

View your complete electronic health records on the go. Access medical history, lab results, prescriptions, and consultation notes anytime.

Secure Login

Your data is protected with advanced security measures including encrypted login, biometric authentication, and secure data transmission.

Compatible Health Devices

The TeleDoctorSA App seamlessly integrates with home health devices, allowing you to track and share vital signs with your healthcare team automatically.

Blood Pressure Monitors

Track systolic & diastolic pressure

Glucose Meters

Monitor blood sugar levels

Pulse Oximeters

Measure oxygen saturation

Digital Thermometers

Record body temperature

Smart Scales

Track weight & BMI

ECG Devices

Monitor heart rhythm

How It Works

1

Open the App

Open the secure TeleDoctorSA patient app in your browser and sign in to your account. No app-store download is required.

2

Connect Your Devices

Pair your health monitoring devices with the app via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi for automatic data syncing.

3

Track Your Health

Take measurements with your devices and watch as data automatically uploads to your health dashboard.

4

Share with Clinicians

Your healthcare team receives real-time updates, enabling proactive care and timely interventions.
