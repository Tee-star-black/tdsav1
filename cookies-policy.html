Cookie Policy

This policy explains how TeleDoctorSA may use cookies, browser storage, and similar technologies when you use our public website.

Last updated: 9 September 2026 TeleDoctorSA · South Africa

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files or similar browser technologies that can be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help a site remember preferences, support essential functions, or understand how visitors use the site.

2. How TeleDoctorSA uses cookies

TeleDoctorSA aims to keep the use of cookies and similar technologies limited and proportionate. Depending on the features available on the website, the following categories may be used:

Essential technologies

These support basic site functions, security, navigation, form behaviour, or remembering a choice that is necessary for the website to work as expected.

Preference technologies

These may remember settings or choices you make so the website can provide a more consistent experience on a later visit.

Analytics technologies

If analytics tools are enabled on the website, they may collect information such as page visits, device type, browser type, approximate location, and interaction patterns. TeleDoctorSA should use this information to understand website performance and improve the user experience, not to make clinical decisions.

3. Third-party services

Some pages may link to or load services operated by third parties. These services may process information according to their own privacy and cookie practices.

  • WhatsApp: when you choose to contact or book with TeleDoctorSA through WhatsApp, you leave the TeleDoctorSA website and interact with a Meta service.
  • Google services: some pages may use Google-hosted fonts, maps, or other Google services. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy documentation.
  • Embedded or external services: other external platforms may receive technical information when you actively load or follow their content.

4. What cookies are not used for

Cookies on the public TeleDoctorSA website should not be used to diagnose medical conditions, make automated treatment decisions, or replace a consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.

Clinical information submitted through a dedicated TeleDoctorSA care platform may be governed by additional privacy, consent, and security terms separate from this public website policy.

5. Managing cookies

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how certain website features work. Browser controls vary, so refer to your browser's privacy or site-data settings for the available options.

6. Changes to this policy

TeleDoctorSA may update this Cookie Policy when website functionality, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

7. Contact

For questions about this Cookie Policy or how information is handled on the TeleDoctorSA website, contact info@teledoctorsa.co.za or use the contact page.
