Cookie Policy

A clear explanation of the browser storage and related technologies that may be used when you visit TeleDoctorSA.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files or similar browser technologies stored on your device when you visit a website. They can support essential site functions, remember preferences, or help website owners understand how visitors use a service.

How TeleDoctorSA uses browser technologies

TeleDoctorSA aims to keep the use of cookies and similar technologies limited and proportionate.

CategoryPurposeTypical status
EssentialNavigation, security, form behaviour and remembering necessary choices.Required for site operation
PreferencesRemembering settings that make repeat visits more consistent.Optional where used
AnalyticsUnderstanding page visits, devices and interaction patterns to improve website performance.Optional where enabled

Third-party services

Some pages may link to or load services operated by third parties. Those services may process information according to their own privacy and cookie practices.

  • WhatsApp: booking or contacting TeleDoctorSA through WhatsApp takes you to a Meta service.
  • Google services: some pages may use Google-hosted fonts, maps, or other Google services.
  • Other external services: external platforms may receive technical information when you actively open or load their content.

Clinical information

Cookies on the public TeleDoctorSA website should not be used to diagnose medical conditions, make automated treatment decisions, or replace consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.

Clinical information submitted through a dedicated TeleDoctorSA care platform may be governed by additional privacy, consent and security terms separate from this public website policy.

Managing your preferences

You can use the Cookie Settings control on this page to review the website preference prompt again. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential browser storage may affect how certain website features work.

Changes to this policy

TeleDoctorSA may update this Cookie Policy when website functionality, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

Contact

For questions about this Cookie Policy or website privacy, email info@teledoctorsa.co.za or use the contact page.
