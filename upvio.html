UPVIO Vitals AI

AI-powered wellness insights, in one guided scan.

UPVIO Vitals AI uses a standard smartphone or web camera to run a non-contact face scan in about 30 seconds, providing wellness-oriented estimates such as heart rate, HRV, breathing rate, blood pressure estimates, and broader wellbeing markers in one guided digital flow.

Wellness use only. This non-invasive, low-risk experience is intended for general wellness insights only. It is not for diagnosis, screening, monitoring, disease management, alerts, or treatment guidance, and it is not a substitute for an FDA-authorized medical device.
