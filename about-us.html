About Dr. Mukudu & Partners

HPCSA-Registered Excellence in Digital Healthcare

TeleDoctorSA operates under the trusted brand of Dr. Mukudu & Partners, a fully registered private medical practice with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA). Our team comprises experienced healthcare professionals committed to delivering exceptional medical care through innovative digital platforms.

With years of combined medical experience and a passion for healthcare technology, we've created a seamless telemedicine ecosystem that maintains the personal touch of traditional consultations while leveraging the convenience and efficiency of modern technology.

All our healthcare practitioners are fully registered, professionally insured, and adhere to the highest ethical and clinical standards set by the HPCSA and South African healthcare regulations.
