Returns & Refunds Policy

Your satisfaction is our priority - Learn about our return and refund process

Last Updated: February 25, 2026

Welcome to TeleDoctorSA Returns Policy

At TeleDoctorSA, we are committed to your satisfaction with our medical devices and healthcare products. This Returns and Refunds Policy outlines the conditions and procedures for returning products purchased through our online shop. Due to the nature of medical devices, certain restrictions and conditions apply to protect the health and safety of all our customers.

Return Eligibility

General Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return, products must meet the following criteria:

  • Timeframe: Returns must be initiated within 30 days of delivery for most products
  • Condition: Products must be unused, unopened, and in original packaging with all seals intact
  • Documentation: Original proof of purchase (order number or receipt) must be provided
  • Completeness: All original accessories, manuals, and packaging must be included

Important: For hygiene and safety reasons, opened or used medical devices cannot be returned or exchanged unless they are defective or damaged upon arrival.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for returns or refunds:

Cannot Be Returned:

  • Opened Medical Devices: Any device that has been opened, used, or unsealed (e.g., blood pressure monitors, glucose meters, pulse oximeters)
  • Disposable Items: ECG sensor patches, electrodes, test strips, lancets, and other single-use consumables
  • 73 Health Subscriptions: Digital subscription services once activated (subject to cancellation policy)
  • Personalized Items: Products that have been customized or configured specifically for you
  • Close-to-Skin Products: Items that come into direct contact with skin or bodily fluids, once opened
  • Sale or Clearance Items: Products marked as final sale or clearance items

Defective or Damaged Products

If your product arrives damaged or is found to be defective:

You Are Covered!

  • Immediate Notification: Contact us within 48 hours of delivery
  • Photographic Evidence: Provide clear photos of the damage or defect
  • Free Replacement: We will replace the product at no cost to you
  • Return Shipping: We cover all return shipping costs for defective items
  • Full Refund Option: If a replacement is not available, we will issue a full refund

Common defects covered include:

  • Manufacturing defects affecting functionality
  • Physical damage during shipping
  • Missing components or accessories
  • Product does not match description or specifications
  • Device fails to power on or function as intended (within warranty period)

Return Process

Follow these steps to initiate a return:

  1. Contact Our Support Team

    Reach out via email (info@teledoctorsa.co.za), phone (+27 78 033 6394), or WhatsApp to request a return authorization

  2. Provide Order Details

    Have your order number, product name, and reason for return ready

  3. Receive Return Authorization

    We will review your request and provide a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number if approved

  4. Package Your Return

    Securely package the product in its original packaging with all accessories and include your RMA number

  5. Ship the Product

    Send the package to the address provided. Use a trackable shipping method and retain your tracking number

  6. Inspection and Processing

    Once received, we will inspect the product and process your refund or replacement within 5-7 business days

Tip: Take photos of the product and packaging before shipping for your records. This can help resolve any shipping disputes.

Refunds

Refund Processing

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund:

  • Approved Returns: Refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 7-10 business days
  • Yoco Payments: Refunds for Yoco transactions typically appear in 3-5 business days
  • Bank Transfers/EFT: Refunds via bank transfer may take 5-10 business days
  • Notification: You will receive an email confirmation once your refund has been processed

Refund Amount

  • Full purchase price of the returned product(s)
  • Original shipping costs (if the return is due to our error or a defective product)
  • Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the product is defective or we made an error

Please Note: If you received free shipping on your original order due to a promotion or order value threshold, the cost of shipping will be deducted from your refund if you return all items.

Exchanges

We offer exchanges in limited circumstances:

Eligible for Exchange

  • Defective products within the warranty period
  • Incorrect items shipped (our error)
  • Damaged products upon arrival

Exchange Process

If you need to exchange an item for the same product:

  1. Contact our support team with your exchange request
  2. Follow the return process outlined above
  3. Once we receive and approve your return, we will ship the replacement at no additional cost

Size/Model Changes: If you wish to exchange for a different size or model, you must return the original product for a refund and place a new order for the desired item.

Subscription Returns & Cancellations

73 Health Nursing Subscriptions

Special terms apply to our 73 Health subscription services:

  • Trial Period: 7-day trial period for first-time subscribers (full refund if cancelled within 7 days)
  • Monthly Subscriptions: Can be cancelled at any time; no refunds for partial months
  • Cancellation: Must provide 7 days notice before next billing cycle
  • Unused Services: No refunds for unused subscription time
  • Verification Fees: Nursing registration verification fees are non-refundable

Professional Tip: You can pause your subscription for up to 2 months instead of cancelling if you need a temporary break.

Warranty Information

All medical devices sold by TeleDoctorSA come with manufacturer warranties:

Warranty Coverage

  • Home Monitoring Devices: 12-month manufacturer warranty
  • ECG247 Professional Equipment: 24-month manufacturer warranty
  • Accessories and Consumables: 90-day warranty against defects

Warranty Claims

To make a warranty claim:

  1. Contact us with your product details and description of the issue
  2. Provide proof of purchase and warranty documentation
  3. We will facilitate the warranty claim with the manufacturer
  4. Repairs or replacements will be handled according to warranty terms

Important: Warranty does not cover damage from misuse, accidents, or normal wear and tear. Always follow the manufacturer's usage instructions.

Consumer Rights

Your rights under the South African Consumer Protection Act (CPA):

  • Cooling-Off Period: You have the right to cancel online purchases within 7 days of receipt (subject to product eligibility)
  • Right to Information: Clear, accurate information about products before purchase
  • Safe Products: All products meet South African safety and quality standards
  • Fair Dealings: Protection against unfair or deceptive practices

However, the CPA cooling-off period does not apply to medical devices once they have been opened or used, due to hygiene and safety considerations.

Shipping Costs

Who Pays Return Shipping?

We Pay:

  • Product is defective or damaged
  • Wrong item was shipped
  • Item doesn't match description

Customer Pays:

  • Change of mind (for eligible unopened items)
  • Ordered wrong size or model
  • No longer needed

Recommendation: Use a trackable shipping service and purchase shipping insurance for high-value returns. We are not responsible for items lost in transit unless the return shipping was arranged by TeleDoctorSA.

Important Notes

  • Inspection Required: All returns are subject to inspection. If the returned product does not meet our return conditions, we reserve the right to refuse the return or charge a restocking fee.
  • Restocking Fees: A 15% restocking fee may apply to unopened items returned due to change of mind
  • Incomplete Returns: Returns missing accessories, manuals, or packaging may be subject to deductions from the refund amount
  • Fraudulent Returns: We reserve the right to refuse returns that appear fraudulent or abusive
  • Policy Changes: We reserve the right to update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date

Need Help with a Return?

Our customer support team is here to assist you with any questions about returns or refunds.
